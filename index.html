SURVIVAL ARENA

Blast slimes. Buy gear. Outlast the Overlord.

Arena background

PvP: first to 3 round wins. Hit your rival to earn coins. Each player has their own shop.

HOW TO PLAY

Goal

Survive waves of slimes, bats, and brutes. Every 5th wave brings the Overlord boss. Between waves, the shop opens.

PvP: fight your rival. Hit them to earn coins. The first to 3 round wins takes the match.

Controls: Player 1

WASD move · Mouse aims · Space or click fires · E picks up or revives · 1-9 switch weapon · Left Shift uses special

Controls: Player 2

Arrow keys move · Auto-aims · Right Shift fires · M picks up or revives · Numpad 0 switches weapon · Enter uses special

Other

P or Esc pauses · N mutes

Pickups

Ammo, healing, and new weapons drop from enemies. Walk over one and press E.

Power-ups (Shield, Rage, Haste) activate automatically when you walk over them.

Weapons

Assault Rifle, Sniper (pierces), Shotgun, Sword, and Knife. Buy them in the shop.
